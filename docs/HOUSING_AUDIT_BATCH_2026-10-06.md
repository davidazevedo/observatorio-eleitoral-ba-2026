# Housing audit batch — checkpoint 2026-10-06

## Purpose

This checkpoint freezes the analytical state of the state-funded 50-unit housing cases investigated in the electoral transfer window. It is an audit map, not a finding of illegality.

The documentary chain used by this batch is:

`payment → procurement → result/adjudication → supplier → contract → service order → physical execution/measurement → legal-exception evidence`

A missing link means only **not located in the sources consulted by the stated cutoff**. It must never be rendered as proof that the act does not exist.

## Legal closure test

For transfers from the State to municipalities during the 2026 restricted period, the ordinary pre-existing-obligation exception is treated as satisfied only when the file supports, cumulatively:

1. a formal instrument existing before the restricted period;
2. physical execution of the relevant work/service already started before 2026-07-04;
3. a pre-fixed execution schedule also preceding that boundary.

A previously published agreement and schedule, without evidence of physical execution before the boundary, are not treated as sufficient by themselves. Emergency/calamity is a separate path and requires its own formal act plus factual/object linkage.

Source baseline: PGE-BA electoral guidance / FAQ and the TSE precedent cited by PGE (RO-El 176880, judgment of 2021-03-25).

## Evidentiary states

- **FACT** — directly supported by a preserved primary/public source.
- **NEGATIVE SEARCH** — a defined query/snapshot returned no matching item/result at the cutoff.
- **INFERENCE** — analytical explanation compatible with the documents but not yet proven by the formal act.
- **OPEN LINK** — a required document in the chain has not yet been located.
- **EXCLUDED OBJECT** — document found, but demonstrably refers to another program/object and is not used to close the state agreement.

## TSE crossmatch policy

A supplier is eligible for electoral crossmatch only after unambiguous documentary identification by CNPJ or equivalent primary-source identifier.

Negative TSE results must include:
- dataset version / collection date;
- exact files queried;
- normalized identifier;
- query method;
- hit count;
- caveat that later dataset versions or other relationship classes may differ.

Nunes Engenharia Ltda. currently satisfies that method and yielded zero exact hits in the preserved BA extracts. No electoral connection is inferred from the absence or presence of a name-only similarity.
